# Nghe Di — Curriculum Outline, Units 2–5

A planning outline for Levels 2–5, continuing the structure-forward, audio-first
design of Level 1. Each level is 30 days (plus reuse of the Day-0 pronunciation
primer). The same lesson architecture applies throughout: **recall → dialogue →
drill → synthesis**, tones + vowel cues on first use, SRS review woven into recall,
and a one-word preview of the next day.

Design principles carried over from Level 1:
- **Structure over vocabulary.** Each level has ~6–8 dedicated `[STRUCTURE]`
  lessons that teach a reusable frame; the rest are scenarios that recombine it.
- **Spiral.** Every level re-drills prior frames in new contexts.
- **Southern (HCMC) register**, comprehensible-input scenes, every word taught or
  glossed before the learner hears it.

Rough CEFR mapping: L1 ≈ A1 (survival) · L2 ≈ A1→A2 · L3 ≈ A2 · L4 ≈ A2→B1 ·
L5 ≈ B1.

## Level 2 — Time, routines & connected speech (A1→A2)

**Goal:** move from single-clause survival lines to talking about *when* things
happen — past, present, future — and stringing clauses together.

**Grammar backbone:** progressive `đang`, future `sẽ`, completed `đã`/`rồi` vs.
`chưa`; when-clauses (`khi`, `lúc`); frequency (`thường`, `hay`, `thỉnh thoảng`,
`luôn`, `không bao giờ`); sequencing (`trước`, `sau`, `rồi`, `sau đó`); joining
(`và`, `với`, `cũng`); comparatives (`hơn`, `bằng`, `nhất`); **colloquial
softeners** (`nha`, `nhé`, `nghen`, `đi`) and **casual question particles**
(`vậy`, `hả`, `à` — "sao vậy?", "gì vậy?").

**Vocabulary domains:** full clock + days/months/dates, daily-routine verbs,
transport, hobbies/free time, extended pronouns/family address.

## Level 3 — People, places & opinions (A2)

**Goal:** describe the world and the people in it, and start expressing
preferences, reasons, and suggestions.

**Grammar backbone:** adjective order (noun + adjective); degree (`rất`, `hơi`,
`khá`, `quá`); suggestions/imperatives (`hãy`, `đừng`, `… nhé`, `mình … đi`);
reason (`vì … nên`); opinion (`tôi nghĩ`, `theo tôi`, `thấy … thế nào`);
location prepositions; possessive & relationships; **emphasis & soft negation**
(`đâu` as in "đâu có" / "không … đâu", `luôn`, `chứ`, `mà` — attitude particles).

**Vocabulary domains:** family, jobs, appearance/personality, house & rooms,
city places, colors, clothing, emotions.

## Level 4 — Problem-solving, modality & conditionals (A2→B1)

**Goal:** handle things going wrong — health, admin, travel, complaints — and
express ability, obligation, and conditions.

**Grammar backbone:** modality (`có thể`/`được` = can, `phải` = must, `nên` =
should, `cần` = need); permission/request (`cho … được không`, `làm ơn`);
conditional (`nếu … thì`); passive (`bị`/`được`) expanded; simple reported speech
(`nói là`, `bảo`); superlatives & `càng … càng`.

**Vocabulary domains:** body & symptoms, doctor/pharmacy, bank/post/admin,
accommodation & transport problems, emergencies, repairs/services, complex
directions.

## Level 5 — Narration, nuance & culture (B1)

**Goal:** tell stories, express nuanced opinions and hypotheticals, and navigate
culture and register like a comfortable intermediate speaker.

**Grammar backbone:** narrative connectors (`đầu tiên`, `sau đó`, `cuối cùng`,
`tuy nhiên`, `mặc dù … nhưng`); wishes (`mong`, `hi vọng`, `ước`); hypothetical/
regret (`giá mà`, `nếu … thì đã`); opinion nuance (`có lẽ`, `chắc`, `đồng ý`,
`không hẳn`); relative clauses (`người/cái/nơi … mà`); emphasis & colloquial
particles (`chứ`, `mà`, `đấy`, `cơ`); register & regional awareness.

**Vocabulary domains:** storytelling, life events, festivals (Tết, đám cưới),

## Notes for implementation

- To build a level, port its 30-row table into `CURRICULUM` in
  `src/lesson_generator/curriculum.py` (same `(level, day): {vocab, scene}`
  shape as Level 1), then hand-author or generate `lessons/L{n}-D*/lesson.json`.
- Keep the SRS review schedule honest: `vocab_db.json` already tracks
  `day_introduced` continuously across levels via `absolute_day`, so reviews
  spill correctly from one level into the next.
- The `[STRUCTURE]` lessons are the backbone — write those first in each level;
  scenarios then recombine known frames + a few new domain words.
- `[STRUCTURE]` count per level (~7) deliberately mirrors Level 1 so the cognitive
  load per level stays roughly constant even as content gets harder.
- **Colloquial particles** (`nha`/`nhé`/`nghen`, `vậy`/`hả`/`à`, `đâu`/`luôn`/
  `chứ`/`mà`, …) are taught as their own `[STRUCTURE]` lessons in L2–L3 and as
  the L5 capstone, then spiralled into later scenarios. Unlike Level 1, where
  they are only glossed in passing, the *taught* particles should be added to
  `vocab_db.json` so the SRS actually drills them. `đâu` (emphatic "no way")
  and `luôn` ("right away/altogether") in particular must be taught explicitly —
  they are not inferable from the question-word `đâu` or any English analogue.
